## Walking doors and furniture

Dry ordinary walking now opens doors or tall gates, tries the opposite swing when needed, and remembers the object to close later. Remembered coordinates belong to the exact NPC generation: same-generation transforms retain them, while despawn/slot reuse clears them. They remain transient and have no world-save or reconnect payload. Beyond a strict two-tile horizontal distance the NPC attempts an unforced close; actor obstruction preserves the memory until the strict four-tile horizontal or vertical abandonment threshold. Closing alone does not force an NPC update. Opening always draws `Next(10)`, including shelter weather/night; successful opening adds 80 timer ticks. Total opening failure reverses facing but preserves horizontal velocity, whereas a missed opening chance follows ordinary obstacle movement.

Shared mutation authority supports true unforced close and the source tall-gate frame geometry: style height `94`, row offsets `0/20/38/56/74`. Existing forced packet-19 actions retain their actor-bypass contract. World mutation precedes replication; an outbound failure does not undo tiles.

A successful chair roll skips the furniture roll even if the chair is absent, reserved or occupied. Chairs use their retained identity, including inactive remnants; active seated NPCs and seated players in slots `0..254` block the seat, including sitting ghosts. The interactable/avoided tile sets are source-pinned catalogs.

Furniture offers now consume the same source nearby danger scan as movement and combat; a hostile elsewhere in the world no longer suppresses a quiet local furniture offer.
