# Fishing crate authority, Terraria 1.4.5.8

The client-validation candidate accepts a complete possible vanilla crate outcome, rather than requiring the client's independent RNG history to match the server's next roll. Payment and legality remain mandatory. The existing inventory before-image, physical quantity ceilings, private funded quantities and world-item allocation transaction remain the owners. No separate crate inventory or reward ledger is introduced. The preliminary Release build and private domain checks below do not establish completed connected/full/Native or official graphical-client acceptance of this candidate.

`VanillaFishingCrate1458` uses one executable semantic grammar for generation and validation of all 26 crate identities. Hardmode follows the crate identity, not the world's current flag. Wooden, iron and golden crates preserve their retry and pending-success rules: mutually exclusive early rares, the independent wooden weapon pool, ore-versus-bar branches and required successful body cannot be replaced with an item allowlist. Biome primaries, companions, independent common groups and trailing extras remain correlated. Remix substitutions and known Skyblock low-tiles conditions use represented world facts. Failed retry iterations emit no grants and retain no choices, so possible-outcome validation recognizes one successful body without seed enumeration; server generation retains its `256`-attempt refusal bound.

The validator consumes at most `16` distinct raw source grants and returns a legal QuickSpawn order. It rejects missing mandatory groups, extra/exclusive items, invalid counts and duplicate raw identities. Matching is bounded at `65536` semantic steps and copies the admitted indexed input once. Natural-prefix reachability is checked separately against the existing source prefix/world metadata; numerically surviving explicit prefixes are not automatically natural outcomes. The validator does not choose a client seed, change the server RNG or prove that the client's choices were statistically unbiased.

Source `ItemSlot.TryOpenContainer` grants rewards before decrementing the crate. The accepted witness is replayed through the existing `Player.GetItem` storage and world-item owner in that order. The last crate therefore still occupies its slot during every reward placement. Coin conversion and copper provenance retain their existing owner. An open Void Bag with an unrepresented residual storage destination refuses the transaction.

There is no vanilla crate-open intent packet. The ordinary client has already chosen rewards and reports overflow `21` before its ordered changed slots `5`, followed by `138`. The existing whole-inventory journal captures the original stock and retains those observations without granting private reward quantities. A payable net decrement of exactly one real crate, the complete possible correlated outcome, reachable prefixes, deterministic storage image and matching source-shaped overflow must all agree before joint adoption. Overflow uses the same journal and existing world allocation preview, including source reservation, position and velocity bounds. An individual `5`, `21`, lone decrement, missing reward or repeated delimiter cannot pay or produce another opening. Conserved cursor/sort/bank operations retain their existing owners. Mixed bank/open cohorts and several openings in one coalesced batch remain outside this boundary.

An invalid candidate is refused; restoring a retained image requires its capture and actor to remain current. Recovery of every interrupted cohort is not claimed. A refusal does not automatically constitute a cheat ban. A new character-file join trusts its initial physical image under the classic model; the first accepted spawn seals existing fishing ceilings across the main inventory and four banks without minting private pickup/copper provenance. Respawn and transfer do not reseed spent stock.

An unlocked non-SSC client ignores its own-slot `5` correction in the directly observed source handler. The lawful path validates and adopts the client's existing image, so it does not depend on replacing a legitimate local roll with a server reroll. Correcting a refused client image still has that source limitation. Outbound bytes alone establish neither GUI adoption nor local character save behavior. SSC and server `.plr` storage are not prerequisites.

The independent original fixture contains 416 reward observations across all identities, eight seeds and two Remix contexts, plus 52 actual last-unit `ItemSlot.TryOpenContainer` observations. Original `Player.OpenFishingCrate` and `QuickSpawnItem/GetItem` supply physical inventory, prefixes and complete RNG state. The configured fixtures use otherwise empty inventory and low-tiles false, so they do not cover every world/client UI history. Historical removal of the wooden coin draw gives 32 source mismatches. The new private linked domain probe additionally compares 3328 old/new generator draw traces with interleaved grant callbacks and validates the 416 original outcomes; its observed maximum is `3091` semantic steps. A private pending-guard omission is detected by the unchanged exclusive-rares assertion. Evidence is `.cache/fishing-client-validation/crate-domain-acceptance.json`. These are domain/component checks; the previous `0d48670b` full/Windows Native/TCP acceptance does not attest the changed app path. See [fishing status](fishing-authority-1458.md) and [work state](../agent-memory/work-state.md).

A trusted host can still request an explicit server-generated operation through `WorldRuntime`. Observation reserves no stock; changed inventory revision or occupation refuses opening. The running loop must drain the command. Cancellation stops waiting and does not prove that an already queued operation was refunded. This host path remains distinct from ordinary client-outcome reconciliation.
