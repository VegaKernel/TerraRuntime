# Paid boss intents and event prerequisites

Client packet `61` cannot create a boss from the multiplayer allow-list alone. The existing player payment owner now admits the represented single-root item intents: Eye of Cthulhu (`43` → NPC `4`), Destroyer (`556` → `134`) and Skeletron Prime (`557` → `127`). Each requires the selected server-funded item, a live eligible player, the retained item/profile context and night. The source items have use time and animation `45`; the shared paid-use receipt prevents a duplicate intent spending twice. Hardmode is not added as an invented item-use prerequisite.

The represented ordinary, unmounted path implements the `1.4.5.8` `NPC.SpawnOnPlayer` sampler: top-left-based, half-open spawn bounds, up to `500` attempts of `50` ordered X/Y offers, exact housing-wall and `2 × 3` tile-space checks, complete active-player screen rejection, final-attempt bypasses and the source candidate retained between outer attempts. Sampling uses a detached copy of the existing shared NPC random stream. `RuntimeNpcStore.NaturalSpawnPreview` retains physical allocation, generation/protection rules, source `Start = 1` and the boss lifetime multiplier `20`.

Before payment, the caller recaptures all `255` source player slots and checks the clock, progression journal, sampled terrain section versions, creation context, NPC census and shared random owner. Every active screen participant also needs an exact retained human equipment/profile capture: unmodeled clientless bodies and equipment `4341`/`5126` refuse because Portable Stool can change height without a mount. All body captures must remain current after sampling and before the debit. Payment, NPC birth and random state are accepted before baseline or packet observers. Reentrant disconnects, replacement generations and repeated requests do not repeat payment or spawn. The exact original occupation's inventory notification is drained even when a later publication callback throws.

The ordinary sampler is checked against the local `NPC.Spawner.GetSpawnArea`, `HasTileSpawnSpace`, `CheckNotSpawningOnScreen`, `NPC.SpawnOnPlayer` and `SpawnBoss`; item gates and timing come from `Player.SummonItemCheck`, `ItemCheck_UseBossSpawners`, `ItemCheck_CheckCanUse` and `Item.SetDefaults`. Unmounted body coordinates follow `Player.ResizeHitbox` and the existing runtime body owner (`20 × 42` pixels). Tests pin draw bounds/order and the `50,000`-draw ceiling, final bypass, retained candidates, the last player slot, payment/refusal, stale context, physical birth lifetime and public packet order. This is bounded source and owner evidence, not a complete official-client boss replay.

Client King Slime, biome-dependent summons, altar/fishing summons, Twins, Prime limbs and Mechdusa (`-16`) remain refused until their complete selected-item and spawn producer is represented. Mounted and secret-seed contexts requiring different sampling remain closed, including Skyblock and Dual Dungeons. Missing difficulty provenance and Journey's unowned difficulty slider also refuse; the paid route requires known Classic, Expert or Master mode. Trusted server spawn operations, natural boss producers and the existing trusted Mechdusa batch remain available. In particular, the retained trusted Mechdusa placement is separate from this exact ordinary paid sampler; it has no new whole-vanilla parity claim.
