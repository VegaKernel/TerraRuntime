## Measured desert and jungle gap — 2026-09-18

The 2026-09-07 tester reports above were qualitative. They are now measured against an independent reference: a Large seed-1458 world generated by the pinned official server, scanned side by side with the TerraRuntime world for the same seed and size. Counts are whole-map tile counts, so they bound how much of each feature exists, not whether its shape is right.

| feature | tile | official | TerraRuntime | share |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Jungle plants | `61` | $43\,307$ | $1\,425$ | $3\%$ |
| Lihzahrd Brick (Jungle Temple) | `226` | $43\,167$ | $2\,041$ | $5\%$ |
| Living Wood | `191` | $5\,287$ | $658$ | $12\%$ |
| Leaf Block | `192` | $5\,145$ | $867$ | $17\%$ |
| Hive | `225` | $11\,861$ | $2\,515$ | $21\%$ |
| Honey | liquid | $3\,039$ | $928$ | $31\%$ |
| Rich Mahogany | `158` | $1\,553$ | $2\,959$ | $191\%$ |
| Demon Altar | `26` | $648$ | $640$ | $99\%$ |
| Mud | `59` | $1\,774\,560$ | $1\,822\,702$ | $103\%$ |
| Jungle Grass | `60` | $362\,155$ | $384\,895$ | $106\%$ |
| Jungle walls | `15/64/158` | $2\,554\,233$ | $2\,580\,562$ | $101\%$ |
| Oasis surface pools | sand-floored | $17$ | $18$ | $106\%$ |

So the jungle's terrain, walls and grass are close, and its contents are not. The single largest visual deficit is jungle plants: the source's `GlowingMushroomPlantsUndergroundAndJunglePlants` pass is a deterministic whole-map scan that places tile `61` above every exposed Jungle Grass cell with no random gate at all, while the runtime samples a bounded number of random columns. That shape difference, not a tuning constant, is why the jungle reads as bare. The temple is present but roughly a twentieth of its size and in a different place. Living Trees and Hives are materially short. Rich Mahogany is the one feature with too much of it.

This table is a bound on presence, not a parity claim in either direction: matching counts do not prove matching shape, and the mud/grass/wall rows above are consistent with the already-closed `E9` rows for Jungle and Mud Caves To Grass rather than evidence about the open `JungleStructure` family.

- [ ] Jungle plants (`61`): replace the sampled placement with the source's whole-map scan. Needs a `WorldGen.PlaceTile` slice for types `61` and `71` and the small `TooManyJunglePlantsNearby` helper; the mushroom half of the same pass additionally needs `TryGrowingTreeByType(5, ...)`, which has no runtime owner yet.
- [ ] Jungle Temple: `WorldGen.makeTemple` graph, rooms, traps and door.
- [ ] Hives and honey: `HiveBiome` placement and volume.
- [ ] Living Trees: `GrowLivingTree` roots, trunk and room.
- [ ] Rich Mahogany overshoot: identify which owner plants it and why it exceeds the source.

Separately, gem trees (types `583..589`) are spread across the whole official map, x `27..8347` and y `603..2189`, $1\,185$ tiles, while TerraRuntime has $70$, all inside the shimmer area. Something other than the Shimmer pass plants gem trees in vanilla and the runtime has no owner for it.
